William “Wolfie” Quenby is a B2B technology consultant, commercial operator and builder based in the United Kingdom.

His experience spans enterprise technology, IT infrastructure, cybersecurity, new business development, market entry and the fast-moving world of applied AI. The common thread is simple: making complicated things useful, communicable and commercially real.

Quenby Technology was built for situations that do not fit neatly inside one discipline. A go-to-market problem may actually be a data problem. A technology decision may fail because the commercial case is unclear. An AI initiative may need less experimentation and more operating design.

The work is direct, collaborative and deliberately hands-on. You will not be handed a deck and wished good luck. The aim is to leave behind a clearer decision, a stronger system and a team better equipped to run it.
